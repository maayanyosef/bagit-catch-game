# Bagit Catch Game

![Bagit](https://emoji.slack-edge.com/T8UPK0YQ3/bagit/130f01dbd0e3f77d.gif)

## Description

Bagit Catch Game is a fun browser-based arcade game where you control a cat to catch falling baguettes. Catch streaks build combos for multiplied points, power-ups shake things up, and a live leaderboard tracks the top players.

## How to Play

### Desktop Controls
1. **Start the Game**: Enter your nickname and click "Start Game"
2. **Move the Cat**: Move your mouse left/right, or use the ← → arrow keys
3. **Jump**: Press **Space** or **↑** to jump
4. **Pause**: Press **P** or **ESC** (or click the Pause button)
5. **Stop**: Click "Stop Game" to quit and return to the menu

### Mobile Controls
1. **Start the Game**: Enter your nickname and tap "Start Game"
2. **Move the Cat**: Use the ← → buttons at the bottom of the screen
3. **Jump**: Tap the ↑ button
4. **Pause**: Tap the Pause button in the top bar
5. **Best experience**: Landscape mode

## Scoring

- **+1 point** per baguette caught (more with combos)
- **-1 point** per baguette missed (floor: 0)
- **⭐ +1 star** every 10 catches
- **⭐⭐ +2 bonus stars** every 100 catches
- **60 seconds** per round

## Combo System 🔥

Catch baguettes consecutively without missing to build your combo multiplier:

| Streak | Multiplier |
|--------|-----------|
| 1–4    | ×1        |
| 5–9    | ×2        |
| 10+    | ×3        |

Combo resets on any miss. Score popups show your multiplier in real time.

## Power-Ups

Special power-ups fall from the sky — catch them for bonuses:

| Power-Up | Effect | Duration |
|----------|--------|----------|
| ⚡ Speed  | Doubles cat movement speed | 5 seconds |
| 🧲 Magnet | Pulls nearby baguettes toward the cat | 6 seconds |
| 💚 +10 pts | Instant +10 points | Instant |

## Difficulty

Baguette fall speed and spawn rate scale up as your score increases — it keeps getting harder the better you play.
